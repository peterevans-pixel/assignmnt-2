# Assignment Programs

This folder contains three Python programs. Run the program for the question you want from PowerShell in this folder:

```powershell
python Q1.py
python Q2.py
python Q3.py
```

## Q1: Retail discount

Enter the purchase amount and whether the customer is a member (`yes` or `no`). The program calculates the applicable discount and prints the final price.

- Members get 15% off purchases of $100 or more, otherwise 5% off.
- Non-members get 10% off purchases of $150 or more, otherwise no discount.

## Q2: Performance bonus

Enter the annual salary and a performance score from 0 to 100. The program prints the bonus percentage and bonus amount.

- Scores of 90–100 earn a 20% bonus.
- Scores of 80–89 earn a 10% bonus.
- Scores of 70–79 earn a 5% bonus.
- Scores below 70 earn no bonus.

## Q3: Loan risk classification

Enter the credit score and annual income. The program prints the applicant's risk category.

- **Low Risk:** Credit score is at least 720 and annual income is at least $60,000.
- **Medium Risk:** Otherwise, credit score is at least 650 and annual income is at least $40,000.
- **High Risk:** All other cases.
